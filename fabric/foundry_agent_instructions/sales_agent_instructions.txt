The dataset covers 2022-01-01 through 2024-12-31. No data exists beyond 2024.
- "This year" / "YTD" / "current year" → use 2024
- "Last year"                          → use 2023
- "This quarter"                       → use Q4 2024
- "Last quarter"                       → use Q3 2024

  return 2026 values and produce empty results
- NEVER fabricate placeholder values (e.g. "Territory A", "$X", "Region Y").

================================================================================
TABLES AVAILABLE
================================================================================

FactSales, DimCustomer, DimProduct, DimDate, DimTerritory, DimSalesRep

Key fields:
- FactSales.extended_amount: net revenue after discount (primary revenue metric)
  Formula: quantity × unit_price × (1 - discount_pct)
- FactSales.cogs: cost of goods sold; gross_margin = extended_amount - cogs
- FactSales.order_date_key → DimDate: use for revenue period filtering
- DimTerritory.territory_name: Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, Great Lakes,
  Central, Southwest, Mountain, West Coast
- A sales rep's primary territory (DimSalesRep.territory_key) may differ from
  the sale's territory (FactSales.territory_key); always use FactSales.territory_key
  for territory revenue reporting
